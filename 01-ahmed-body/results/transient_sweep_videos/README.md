# Transient sweep videos (illustrative)

Pressure coefficient and streamwise velocity (Ux/U_inf) on a vertical cut plane (y = 0.10 m) through the body, for the 10, 20, 30 and 35 degree rear-slant transient runs. Frames are 1 ms of simulated time apart and are played at 12 fps, 83 times slower than real time. Frame 0 is the uniform field before the impulsive start.

These runs belong to the first sweep, which was audited afterwards: the body sits on the floor without the 50 mm clearance and the inlet turbulence is far above the experiment's (see docs/errata.md, E2 and E4). The videos illustrate flow structure and are not validated results. They contain no measured (ERCOFTAC) data.

Rendered with scripts/visualization/make_slice_video.py.

Files: ahmed_slant10_transient.mp4, ahmed_slant20_transient.mp4, ahmed_slant30_transient.mp4, ahmed_slant35_transient.mp4
